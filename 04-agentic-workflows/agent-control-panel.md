## What Juno Is Doing

Insights synthesis: every Monday, Juno compares last week's customer signals from Jira, Slack and Notion against the 6-week baseline and drafts a Notion report of what's new, growing, fading, or showing up in a new segment.

## Four Levers

**Stop Conditions** (Loop):

- Max steps: 25 model turns and 150 tool calls per run (a normal week uses ~12 turns / ~80 calls).
- Abort: the same tool errors 3x in a row on the same call (an error or timeout, not an empty or negative result). Source outages follow the 30-min retry rule.
Hard timeout: weekly run targets 60 min (06:00–07:00); hard stop at 07:10 → publishes verified themes as a partial report and DMs "Report delayed". On-demand: 5 min for periods up to 15 days.

**Structured Tool Outputs** (Tools):

- search_jira / read_slack_threads / query_notion_database → OK: {items[], count, source, period}. FAIL: {status: "error", source, reason}. Count = 0 for a whole source in a normal week = "failed", not "quiet week".
- read_jira_issue → OK: {key, description, comments, account, labels}. FAIL: {status: "not_found" | "error"}.
- lookup_account_segment → OK: {account, segment}. No match: {segment: "Unmapped"}, never a guess.
- get_baseline_themes → OK: {weeks_found, themes[{name, count, segments, accounts}]}. weeks_found < 1 = "failed" (no comparison possible).
- verify_quote → {match: true | false, source_ref}. false = quote removed.
- compute_theme_labels → {theme, label: new | growing | fading | new_segment | stable, rule_applied}.
- create_notion_report → OK: {page_url, status: "Draft"}. FAIL: {status: "error", reason}.
- send_slack_dm → OK: {delivered: true, recipient_id}. FAIL: {delivered: false, reason}.

**Confidence Thresholds** (Verification):

- High: theme has ≥2 verified quotes, label matches the compute_theme_labels rule, no duplicate flag → goes into the draft as is.
- Medium: <2 verified quotes, a quote was removed, a possible duplicate, or a label overridden by the rule → goes into the draft tagged "Low confidence: needs review"; the PM checks before sharing.
- Low: >30% of quotes fail, >25% of signals unthemed, or >10 themes changed → Juno creates the report as Draft only, with a banner saying which check failed and by how much. The DM gives the reason and the draft link so the PM reviews it manually. Not used as baseline until reviewed.

**North Star** (Context):

Always: report only what changed in signals since last week (new, growing, fading, new segment), with every claim backed by a verified quote linked to its source.
Never: count Juno's own reports or internal opinions as evidence, guess an account's segment, or send anything to anyone except the pre-configured user.

## Rules of Engagement

**Agency Permission:**

Can read sources, group themes, create the Notion report as Draft and DM the pre-configured user. Cannot share the report, create Jira tickets, or edit sources, past reports or account segments.

**Access Control:**

Read-only on Jira CSUPPORT and PREQ, two public Slack channels (#cs-escalations, #feedback-enterprise) and four Notion databases (Customer Interviews, QBR/Account Notes, Sales Call Notes, Account Segments), plus past Juno reports. 
No Slack DMs, private channels or Notion drafts/personal pages. The only DM Juno can send goes to the pre-configured user.

**Fallback Protocols:**

Jira, segment table or baseline down after 30 min of retries → no report, DM the reason. Slack or Notion down → report with a "missing source" banner. Limit reached → partial report with a banner. Failed checks → Draft with a "manual review" banner.

**Checkpoints:**

The PM reviews every Draft before it's shared, and only reviewed reports become next week's baseline. On-demand runs over 30 days need the PM's OK first.
